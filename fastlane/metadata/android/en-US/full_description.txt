Meet OSS Weather — a lightweight, privacy-first weather app built from open-source components to give you fast, clear and trustworthy forecasts without tracking or bloat.

Beautiful, simple, and fully customizable OSS Weather brings the essentials and the extras you actually use: instant current conditions, hour-by-hour and 10-day forecasts, interactive radar and weather maps, home screen widgets, severe weather alerts, and themes that fit your style.

Key features

* Local current conditions: temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind, pressure, visibility
* Hourly and daily forecasts: plan your day and week with clear timelines
* Interactive maps & radar: precipitation, clouds, radar overlays and zoomable maps
* Severe weather & push notifications: timely alerts for your location (when enabled)
* Home screen widgets: configurable sizes and layouts for quick glanceability
* Multiple units & locales: metric/imperial, 12/24-hour time, language-aware formatting
* Offline caching & efficient updates: view recent data when connectivity is limited
* Customizable UI: light/dark themes, adjustable refresh intervals, compact or detailed layouts
* Privacy-first: no user tracking, minimal permissions required (location & network only for local forecasts)
* Open source on GitHub: inspect, fork, contribute — https://github.com/Akylas/oss-weather

Why choose OSS Weather?

* Transparency: the whole app is open-source — review the code, suggest improvements, or add features.
* No surprises: we don’t collect analytics or sell your data. Location is used locally to provide accurate forecasts and alerts.
* Lightweight and fast: designed to deliver the weather information you need quickly and with low battery impact.

Permissions

* Location (approx. or precise): optional — used to show nearby forecasts and enable local alerts.
* Network / Internet access: required to fetch forecasts, maps, and updates. All other permissions are minimized; no analytics or ad-tracking permissions are included.

Getting started

* Grant location permission (optional) or choose a city manually.
* Add a widget for quick glances from your home screen.
* Customize units, theme and refresh frequency in Settings.
